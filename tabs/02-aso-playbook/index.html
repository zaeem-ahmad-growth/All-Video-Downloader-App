    Keyword board ·

    Keyword opportunity board, relevance first

    Priority = relevance² × opportunity. Relevance blends intent tier (55%), how much of the top 10 is downloader apps (35%) and whether your competitors rank there (10%). Opportunity blends non-brand apps in the top 10, autocomplete demand, the median install proxy and the entry bar — the smallest non-brand app in the top 10. ™ platform name marks keywords that name another company’s product.

    Demand comes from Play’s own autocomplete: the fewer letters before Play suggests the phrase, the higher the score. The volume proxy is the median installs of the top 10. YouTube keywords are left off the board entirely, as are autocomplete phrases with adult-site or piracy intent.

    Best practices · video downloaders

    What works in this category

    Title · 30 chars

    Leaders lead with “Video Downloader” and add one differentiator: HD, private, player, story saver. A title identical to another app’s — as yours is today — splits clicks and invites confusion.

    Short description · 80 chars

    Name the job (save videos from a link) and the extras the title can’t hold. Your editor, MP3 extraction and vault are features almost no competitor lists.

    Full description · 4,000 chars

    Fully indexed. Keep “video downloader” near 2–3%, add a how-to section, and close with a copyright notice and a non-affiliation statement — both are standard in this category.

    Screenshots

    Your listing uses 4 of 8 phone slots. The editor and the vault are the strongest proof points and are not yet shown as framed store screenshots.

    Events & offers

    None of the 16 competitors runs an event or offer card in the four markets checked. A dated card (a feature launch, a trial) is uncontested promotional space.

    Policy

    No platform logos, no “official”, no implied endorsement. Keep platform names out of the title and short description; name supported sites only factually in the full description.

    Intellectual Property policy · Metadata policy

    Watch-outs

    • Platform names are high-volume and high-risk“instagram video downloader”, “reels downloader” and similar searches carry real demand, but naming a platform in the title or short description risks removal for impersonation or trademark misuse.
    • YouTubeYouTube’s terms forbid downloading, and Play removes apps that facilitate it. YouTube keywords are excluded from this board; keep “YouTube” out of every field.
    • “No watermark” searchesPromising to strip a creator’s watermark reads as removing attribution. The keyword is tracked for evidence but not targeted.
    • “Without ads”“video downloader without ads” scores well, but your app contains ads. A claim the listing can’t back is a misleading-claims violation.
    • Invisible audit competitorsSky Vision, Vidow, Vidpal and the other audit apps barely rank on these keywords in any market. Their installs come from elsewhere (paid acquisition, older ranking history), so they are feature references, not keyword references.
    • English-only market dataThe seven non-US markets were searched in English. Local-language boards (Portuguese, German, Spanish, Italian, Arabic, Hindi) are needed before localizing.
